Featured Research

LERTQA-RAG: Can Structured Validation Make Large Language Models Reliable for Multi-Paper Scientific Reasoning?

Humphrey Montalban, Armielyn Obinguar 2025–2026 RAG · NLP · LLM Reliability

We introduce LERTQA (Loop-Enhanced Retrieval with Trust and Quality Assessment), a general-purpose reliability framework that wraps any retrieve-then-generate pipeline with a lightweight validation and correction loop. On 150 topic-balanced queries across five academic domains scored by five independent LLM judges, LERTQA raises overall quality from 0.221 to 0.891, improves faithfulness from 0.123 to 0.990, and reduces hallucinations by 98.3% — while cutting latency by 47%.
